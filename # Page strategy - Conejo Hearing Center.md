**\# Page strategy**

\*\*Conejo Hearing Center · Approved November 19, 2026\*\*

**\#\# Objective and audience**

Generate calls to \*\*818-214-8397\*\* to schedule a hearing test at the Westlake Village office. Texting offers another way to reach the same number. \*\*Message Us\*\* opens the supplied appointment form in a dialog.

The page serves adults who want their hearing checked, people comparing professionally fitted hearing aids, and family members helping someone find care. A ready-to-call visitor needs the service, location, and phone action in the first screen. Someone still deciding needs to know what a test can clarify, who will see them, and how the clinic can help if hearing aids are appropriate.

The approved direction is a compact appointment page with dedicated Hearing Services and Meet Your Audiologists sections. Start with local relevance, explain the two services, put real faces to the care, show patient proof, resolve practical questions, and close with a call.

**\#\# Search intent and ad continuity**

The approved primary intent is \*\*finding a local audiologist for a hearing test and, if appropriate, hearing aid selection and fitting\*\*. “Hearing test Westlake Village,” “hearing aid fitting Westlake Village,” and “audiologist Westlake Village” are AI-generated candidates. The Ads team has yet to supply actual keywords, ad copy, geo-targeting, or an offer. Westlake Village reflects the verified office location.

The click expectation is a nearby appointment with an audiologist. The hero names both services and Westlake Village, the service section explains why the evaluation is the first step, and the primary CTA stays tied to scheduling by phone. Test-first visitors can act immediately. Hearing aid shoppers get specific fitting proof in the next section. Symptom-aware visitors see a familiar example and a next step.

Use this page for local evaluation and professional fitting traffic. Repair, tinnitus, and model-specific searches need their own destination if targeted. Price-led and free-test traffic need a verified price or offer. Check the eventual ads against the hero and CTA before launch. Use the brief's phone number unless the Ads team confirms a tracking arrangement.

\#\# Conversion details

\- The main call button appears in the hero, after reviews, and at the close. The desktop header keeps the phone action accessible. On mobile, the bottom Call button stays visible outside the form dialog.  
\- Opening \*\*Message Us\*\* is a deliberate action. The dialog briefly tells visitors they can ask about an appointment or send a question, then shows the supplied form. Include the call or text route if the embed cannot load. Confirm popup behavior because the supplied snippet specifies an always-shown sidebar.  
\- Keep both service blocks short, with enough room for one specific detail and its practical benefit. Save secondary details for the FAQ. A quick scan of the headings should communicate service, provider, experience, and next step.  
\- The reviews section and hero badge depend on verified Google materials. Keep placeholders internal until the rating, count, and excerpts are available.
